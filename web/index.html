Android Time HA Client

Sends finished Google Assistant / Clock timers and alarms from a tablet to Home Assistant.

Download the APK
  1. Open this page on the tablet and download the APK. Allow installs from your browser when Android asks.
  2. Open the app, tap Open notification access settings, and switch it on.
  3. In Home Assistant, go to Settings → Devices & services. The tablet appears as a discovered device — confirm it and enter the pairing code from the app.
  4. Say “Hey Google, set a 5 second pasta timer” and watch the log at the bottom of the app.

Requires the ha_timer_bridge custom integration in Home Assistant. Timers and alarms arrive as separate events, so you can notify on one without the other.
